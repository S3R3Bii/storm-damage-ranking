# Storm-damage rankings under incomplete reporting

An analyst using storm-damage tables needs to know whether a regional ordering reflects broad evidence or a few extreme and incompletely recorded reports. This study will compare six neighboring states under fixed blank-damage, tail-cap and resampling conventions.

**Protocol stage: no damage result or ranking yet.** Calibration is fixed to 2015–2019 and evaluation to 2020–2023. The five fixed county-designated event types are Flash Flood, Flood, Hail, Thunderstorm Wind and Tornado. The primary score is an equal-year mean share within Illinois, Indiana, Michigan, Minnesota, Ohio and Wisconsin. It is not true risk, expected insured loss, loss per exposed asset or a forecast.

NOAA describes damage values as broad estimates and warns that coverage and reporting are incomplete. The comparison will keep recorded blanks distinct from explicit zeros, retain every declared scenario, preserve episode structure and disclose the assumptions behind resampling. It cannot identify actual missing losses. Full source narratives and unnecessary location/person fields stay outside public history.

See the [fixed protocol](PROTOCOL.md), [source context](SOURCES.md) and [data rights](DATA_RIGHTS.md). Source and coverage gates must pass unchanged. Formal-publication projects, including the separate Azure optimization study, stay private.
