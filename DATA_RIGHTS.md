# Data reuse and publication boundary

Source: NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information / National Weather Service Storm Events Database. Attribution does not imply endorsement. NOAA/NWS public statistical content is reused under the published public-domain/open-data terms, subject to stated exceptions. We do not claim copyright in NOAA data. Selection, parsing, scenarios, resampling, figures and interpretation are our independent research and must not be represented as official NOAA products.

Keep complete annual downloads outside Git. The public source subset contains only source row number, episode/event IDs, state, year, begin year-month, event type, county/zone designator, forecast-office code and property-damage token. No narrative, coordinate, fatality/location-table, injury/death, name or private record is published. The selected report-level numerical fields are official aggregate weather statistics, not person-level records. Retain original-file and minimized-file hashes, extraction rules and an independent original-to-subset certificate.

This permission covers this independent program study and its reviewed manuscript after checks. Separate formal-publication manuscripts, including Azure optimization, and third-party papers remain excluded.
